**The CMake presets are named for the operating system, and CI builds with them.** `debug` and `release` are now `linux-debug`, `linux-release`, `macos-debug`, `macos-release`, `windows-msvc-debug` and `windows-msvc-release`, with a `-gui` twin of each that requires Qt. `coverage`, `coverage-unix` and `tracy` become `linux-coverage`, `macos-coverage`, `windows-llvm-coverage`, `linux-tracy` and `windows-msvc-tracy`, and `perf` is folded into the release presets. A preset appears only on the system it names and builds in `build/<preset>`. The CI jobs configure, build and test through the presets and take Boost from `BOOST_ROOT`; the profiling job gets vcpkg from the shared action at the baseline in `vcpkg.json`.
