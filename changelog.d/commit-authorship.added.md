The repository has a single committer. Every commit must be authored by the
maintainer, and neither commit messages nor files may carry `Co-authored-by`,
`Signed-off-by` or any other trailer, a "generated by/with" statement, a robot
marker or AI/tool attribution.

The rule is enforced by `.githooks/commit-msg` at commit time,
`tools/check_commit_message.py` on demand and the `Commit authorship` CI job
for every push and pull request.
