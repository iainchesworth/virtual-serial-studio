**Stopping a hub releases its idle endpoints.** `hub::stop()` let go of the io_context's work guard before it posted the shutdown handlers to the endpoints, so with nothing else queued the worker threads left at once and the handlers never ran. An endpoint with a parked read then kept itself alive through its transport after the hub was gone, which AddressSanitizer reported as a leak in the 12 unit tests that use a loopback port. The guard now stays until `join()`, and a test checks that destroying the hub frees an idle endpoint.
