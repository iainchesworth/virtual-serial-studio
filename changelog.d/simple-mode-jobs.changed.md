**Simple mode is now a guided list of jobs.** Instead of the panels and the
canvas, Simple mode shows the setups you have made on the left and, on the
right, "What do you want to do?" with jobs in plain words: give a program a
serial port, connect two programs, use or share a device, try a device from
this window, let other computers use it, put several on one line, send several
into one, and let programs take turns. Each job is one form with a picture of
what it builds and a sentence saying what will happen. Each setup then has its
own page that says which port to open in which program, shows whether the
program has opened it, warns when its baud rate differs from the device's, and
lets you type to the device and see the data going through. Change, add or
close a program, and Remove setup work from that page. Advanced mode keeps the
canvas and panels, and both modes show the same setups.
