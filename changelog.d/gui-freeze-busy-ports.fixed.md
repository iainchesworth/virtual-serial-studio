- **The window no longer freezes ("Not responding") while ports are busy.** With a loopback linked to a fixture that sent and echoed bytes, every batch of traffic made the window ask the operating system about the driver, the serial devices and the firewall several times over, tell every pane that the ports and links had changed because a byte counter moved, look at the engine twice, reset the whole Monitor list and handle a whole backlog of events in one go, so the window could be away from its event loop for a second or more. Now the setup readiness is reused for a few seconds, a change of byte counters is a signal of its own that only redraws the numbers, the engine is looked at once per burst, the Monitor adds and removes rows instead of resetting, and a flood of events reaches the window in slices with a pause between them so input and painting keep their share.
