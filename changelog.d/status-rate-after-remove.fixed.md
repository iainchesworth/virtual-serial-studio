**The status bar no longer shows a huge transfer rate after a port is removed.** The rate came from the difference of two byte totals summed over the ports that exist, and removing a port with traffic made the total fall, so the unsigned difference wrapped to about 1.8e19 and the rate was converted out of range (found by UBSan in the UI journeys). A fall now counts as no traffic, and the rate text is clamped.
