**A port that lost its connection can be opened again, and lost driver ports can be recreated.** The engine gains `endpoint::reopen`, which swaps a new transport under an endpoint and keeps its name, id and links, and `studio::reopen`, which builds that transport from how the port was made. `vss_cli run --reconnect` opens a dropped `tcp://` or `rfc2217://` client again after a delay that doubles from one second to thirty (`studio::set_reconnect_policy` sets it). When the helper service or the driver restarts and removes the Windows driver ports under your programs, the window shows a "Some COM ports were lost" banner with a **Recreate ports** button that makes them again with the COM numbers they asked for.
