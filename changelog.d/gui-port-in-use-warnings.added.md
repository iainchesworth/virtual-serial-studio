**Studio warns before it takes a port away from a program that has it open.** Removing a port, starting a new setup and closing the window now say which ports programs have open (as **Waiting for program** and **Program connected** are known: a pseudo terminal on Linux, a Studio driver port on Windows) and that those programs may stop or show an error. Nothing is asked when no program is known to hold a port, and a port Studio cannot observe is never claimed to be in use.
