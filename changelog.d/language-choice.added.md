**The application chooses its own language.** At start-up it takes the system's preferred languages in order, matches each by its language alone (any `en-*` is English, `iw` and `ji` are Hebrew and Yiddish, Chinese is Simplified unless the tag says Traditional) and loads the first catalogue it has, instead of leaving the choice to the platform's translator search. Hebrew, Yiddish and Arabic set a right-to-left layout. The About window and the diagnostic bundle show the language chosen and the languages the system offered, and `VSS_LANGUAGE` names one to try. No catalogue ships yet; the build takes catalogues from `apps/vss_gui/translations/` as they arrive.
