**The Studio window can choose and change the COM number of a Studio driver port.** On Windows with the driver and its helper service, **Add port** has a **COM number** field (Automatic takes the lowest free number) for one port at a time, and the Inspector shows the COM number of a driver port with a **Change...** button. A number that another device has, a missing helper service, a program holding the port, or the driver being full is refused beside the number, in words that say what to do, and nothing is changed. The change restarts the port, which takes a second or two, so it runs while the window stays usable. The number is saved with the setup.
