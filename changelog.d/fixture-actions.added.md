**Send, repeat and monitor from a port's menu, and fixture behaviours.** Right-clicking a port in the Ports list or the Topology offers Send bytes, Start or Stop repeat, and Open in monitor. A mock fixture in Advanced mode also has an Inspector card with Echo and a byte generator (text line, fixed pattern, counter or random bytes at a set rate), and the same actions in its menu. Sending from a fixture now makes the fixture speak: the bytes travel along its links, through the new `vss::studio::inject_data` and `vss::studio::set_fixture_echo`.
