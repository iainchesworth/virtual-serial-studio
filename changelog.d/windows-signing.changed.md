**Windows programs are signed in release builds, and the permission prompts say what they are for.** The release package's `vss_gui.exe`, `vss_cli.exe` and any `vss_*.dll`, and the helper service of the driver package, are signed and timestamped by the shared signing step when `SIGN_WINDOWS` is on and its secrets exist, and each leg then checks the signatures and fails if one is missing. Without the secrets the step says so and the files stay unsigned. Studio never asks for administrator rights when it starts. Before the two steps that do, Restart as administrator and Allow in Windows Firewall, the window now explains why Windows will ask and what the prompt shows.
