**Studio can act as an RFC 2217 server.** In the Network endpoint dialog, choose RFC 2217 and "Wait for other computers to connect (server)", or attach `rfc2217-listen://host:port`. A serial-over-network client such as pySerial's `rfc2217://` can then open the endpoint as a serial port: data passes through, the client's DTR and RTS reach the linked port as control lines, and the modem lines that reach the endpoint are sent back to the client. The baud rate and framing the client asks for are accepted but are not applied to a real device linked to the server. The ports library gains `rfc2217-listen://` and `vss::rfc2217::session`.
