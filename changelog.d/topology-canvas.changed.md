**Topology nodes can be moved, and links go around them.** Drag a node with the mouse or move it with the arrow keys and its links follow. The layout keeps nodes apart and routes a link around a node instead of under it, and a new node goes into free space next to the node it connects to. Fit shows everything with a margin from any zoom or scroll position, and a resize re-fits until you zoom, scroll or move a node and then keeps your view. The canvas menu gains Tidy layout and Fit.
