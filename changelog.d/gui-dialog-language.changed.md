**The dialogs follow the new design language.** Dialog footers lose their grey band and divider line and share the 24 px dialog padding, so buttons and footer notes no longer touch the window edge. Setup check and "Why is nothing arriving?" show their checks as one grouped list with tinted status tiles, and a check's title no longer wraps after a word or two. Settings lists its sections on the dialog surface with the current one as a tinted pill. Keyboard shortcuts no longer clips the longer key caps, and its column headings are left-aligned small capitals. The Benchmark result figures sit on raised tiles in a grouped section, and the Wiring inspector's title stays on one line.
