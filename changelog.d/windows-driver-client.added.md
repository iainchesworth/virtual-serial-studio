**The studio can open the hub end of a Windows driver port.** `vss::driver_available()` now lists the driver's ports instead of probing the registry, and `vss::create_driver_port()` takes a free port, opens its hub end for overlapped I/O and returns a transport that carries its bytes and control lines. The port's DTR, RTS and break show as the lines the application drives, and CTS, DSR, carrier detect and ring can be set. It reports `unsupported` when there is no driver and `device_busy` when every port is taken. With the driver installed, an `auto` virtual port on Windows now prefers it to com0com. The ports are the devices that were created; creating them on demand is a later step.
