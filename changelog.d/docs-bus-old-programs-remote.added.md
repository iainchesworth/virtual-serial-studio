**The user guide covers RS-422 and RS-485, old Windows programs and devices on other computers.** There are new sections on how RS-232, four-wire RS-422 or RS-485 (a split) and two-wire RS-485 (a shared bus) are modelled and what a bus does not do (it does not report two stations sending at once, hold the line for a turn-around time or carry RTS); on old programs (32-bit programs, the `COM3` and `\\.\COM12` forms of a name, fixed COM numbers, and programs that try every port); and on putting a device from another computer on a bus safely (`allow=`, an SSH tunnel or a VPN). The scenarios page has matching recipes.
