**The desktop app has a new design language.** The window is a tinted ground,
and buttons are fills instead of grey outlines: a blue primary, a soft grey
secondary, and plain-text ghost and quiet buttons. Fields are the only outlined
controls, and they show a blue edge when focused. Chips and RX, TX and DROP
tags are tinted capsules without borders, with TX in violet. The Simple and
Advanced switch and other segmented controls are a track with a raised thumb.
Tables have no grid lines or striped rows, and tabs are pills. The accent is a
clear blue, and the dark theme is a neutral graphite instead of navy. Every
pair of colours still meets WCAG AA contrast.
