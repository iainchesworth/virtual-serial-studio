**A capture can now be played back into a port.** `vss_cli play FILE (--uri=URI | --port=NAME) [--speed=X] [--loop] [--direction=R|W] [--from=NAME]` reads a `.cap` or JSON Lines capture and writes the records of one direction into one port at the times they were recorded, so a recorded master or device can drive a bus without the program that made the recording. `R` records (the default) are the bytes the recorded port received; `W` records are the bytes written to it. A loop lasts as long as the capture did. The studio library gains `vss::replay_player`, which sends with the port's own write and a steady timer, so the times are absolute and do not drift.
