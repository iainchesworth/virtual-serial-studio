The desktop app has a visual and theming pass. Icons now come from an icon
engine that draws at any size and display scale in the colour the theme has
at that moment, and every action, button and field that the design gives an
icon shows it: Remove port, Remove link, Copy, Pause and Resume, Send, Run,
Refresh, Export and the search box, and a disclosure shows the right chevron
when closed and the down chevron when open. The Simple and Advanced switch in
the status bar no longer sits under the window's rounded corner. Text is
sharper: every size is a whole number of pixels, the unhinted outline is used,
display scales such as 125 and 150 percent are not rounded, and the fixed
font on Windows is Consolas instead of Courier New. Buttons,
menus, tabs and dock buttons have hover and pressed fills from two new colour
tokens, corners are rounder, the combo box and spin box arrows match the icon
set, and focus shows on text fields.
