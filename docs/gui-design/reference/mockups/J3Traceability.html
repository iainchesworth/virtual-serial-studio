Measures and accessibility checks
Measures. Task success, time on task, errors, and the System Usability Scale. Target 80 or higher, against a commonly cited average of 68.
Keyboard only. Every task done without a pointer, focus never lost or hidden.
Screen readers. Narrator or NVDA on Windows, VoiceOver on macOS, Orca on Linux.
Scale. 100 and 200 percent display scale, 200 percent text size, light, dark and high contrast.
